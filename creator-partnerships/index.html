Creator partnerships

You made the spark. We build the business around it.

You bring the original work and the fans. We bring the team, the tech and the launch, and we stay to run it with you, so you can keep creating while it earns.

01You get

What you get

  • A dedicated team for strategy, design, development and launch
  • An official project you approve, credited to you
  • Your share of everything the project earns, set out in writing
  • Ongoing community management and development after launch
  • No tech knowledge needed: we explain everything in plain English
  • No upfront cost: it costs you nothing to partner with us
02We ask

What we ask of you

  • That the work is yours, or that you hold the rights to it
  • A short call to understand your work and your audience
  • Your review and approval of anything carrying your name
  • A clear agreement before anything is built or launched

Straight answers

Before you book.

Q1Crypto

Is this crypto?

Often, yes, but you don't need to understand any of it. Many projects are built around a digital coin that people can buy into, from our community to crypto traders who love a great story, which is where the earning comes from. Others are websites, apps or fan platforms. We'll walk you through it on the first call.

Q2Risk

Is it risky?

Digital coins are speculative: values can rise fast and fall fast, and nobody can promise an income. We're straight with you about that, and you get the risks in writing before you commit to anything.

Q3Our share

How do we get paid?

Usually through an agreed share of what the project earns, so we only win when you win. Every term is written down before work starts.

Q4Stepping away

What if I change my mind?

The agreement sets out how either side can step away. Until it's signed, you're under no obligation at all.

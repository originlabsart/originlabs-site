Risk disclosure

Crypto-assets, including memecoins and other digital tokens, are high risk and highly speculative. Their prices can rise and fall sharply, and you could lose all the money you put in. They are generally not covered by financial compensation or ombudsman schemes.

Nothing on this website is financial, investment, legal or tax advice, or an offer or invitation to buy any crypto-asset. Examples of other projects describe historical events reported by third parties. They are not projects run by Origin Labs unless we say so, and they do not indicate future outcomes. No income, return or level of trading activity is guaranteed for any creator, partner or holder.

In the United Kingdom, communications that promote crypto-assets to consumers are subject to the Financial Conduct Authority's financial promotion rules. This website describes our creative and technology services and does not promote any specific crypto-asset.

Privacy notice

Who we are

Origin Labs (company details to be added) is the controller of personal data collected through this website. Contact: partnerships@originlabs.art.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, link to your work, creator type and message.
  • Chat assistant: if you message Evie, our AI assistant, what you type is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic or Cloudflare) to generate a reply. We don't store chat messages on our servers; your browser keeps the conversation only until you close the tab. Don't share sensitive personal information in the chat.
  • Bookings: if you book a call, Calendly processes your name, email, chosen time and any answers you give, under its own privacy policy.
  • Partnership research: we may research publicly available professional information about creators whose work we admire, such as published business contact details, before getting in touch.

Why, and our legal basis

We use this information to respond to enquiries, arrange and hold calls, and explore potential partnerships. This is based on our legitimate interests in running and growing our business, or on steps you ask us to take before entering a contract.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to stop contacting you, or for a copy, correction or deletion of the data we hold about you, by emailing us. We keep enquiry data only as long as needed for those purposes. In the UK you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

What a great story can do

These stories hit billions. Most of their creators got nothing.

A dog in a hat. A squirrel. A frog from a comic. The bigger the story, the bigger it can go. The people who made the original were usually left on the sidelines, and plenty of the developers who launched these coins sold out for pocket change before the big run.

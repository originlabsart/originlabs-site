Why it works

Coins have already paid animal charities six figures. The ones left out got 0%.

When a charity is part of a coin, the money reaches the animals. When strangers launch a coin around an animal, the people caring for it get nothing from the launch. With Origin Labs, your cause launches the official one, and your share is agreed in writing first.
